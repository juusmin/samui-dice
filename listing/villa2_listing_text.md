## Guest access
The entire villa is yours alone – all four bedrooms, the living and dining area, kitchen, pool, pool lounge, outdoor shower and garden. Nothing is shared and there are no other guests on the property. Private parking is behind the gate.

Our team welcomes you personally on arrival, shows you around the villa and explains everything you need, from the air conditioning to the Wi-Fi. Our pool and garden team visits twice a week; we always let you know in advance and keep their visits short to protect your privacy.

## Interaction with guests
We are an experienced hospitality team living on Samui, working with trusted local partners, and we look after every stay personally – from your first message to check-out.

• Before arrival: we help you plan your trip and arrange your airport pick-up.
• During your stay: we are always a message away and respond quickly, and someone from our team is nearby if anything needs attention on site.
• Local tips: our favourite restaurants, beaches, cafés and markets – and anything practical, from pharmacies to doctors.
• On request: airport and island transfers, car or scooter rental delivered to the villa, island and boat tours, restaurant bookings and special surprises for birthdays or anniversaries.

We are there whenever you need us – and we give you all the privacy you want when you don't.
