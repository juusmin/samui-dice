# Villa 2 – Airbnb listing text (Listing 1, 4 bedrooms)

## Title
Swim-Up Bar Pool Villa · 4BR · Near Choeng Mon

## Description
🏝️ A modern tropical villa where everything centres on your very own private pool oasis. Start the day with a swim, spend the afternoon in the shaded pool lounge with refreshing drinks from the mini bar, and the evening around the dining table with wide sliding doors open to the garden. Four air-conditioned bedrooms, a fully equipped Western kitchen and a walled garden are our villa features that offer space, tranquility and privacy, and everything just minutes from Samui’s Choeng Mon Beach⛱️



## Your property
🏊‍♂️ The heart of the villa is the L-shaped saltwater pool – around 31 m³ of water, 1.4 m deep – with swim-up stools built right into the water. A few steps below the waterline sits the pool lounge – shaded, cooled by a ceiling fan, with a mini bar for ice-cold drinks 🧊. And after a day at the beach, the outdoor rain shower is waiting 🚿

🛋️👩‍🍳 Floor-to-ceiling sliding doors open the spacious living room fully to the pool. The solid-wood dining table seats eight, right next to a modern kitchen island. The Western kitchen is fully equipped with a large fridge, oven, gas hob, air fryer and coffee maker – everything you need to cook your own pad kra pao 🌶️😋

🛌 Three king bedrooms and one queen bedroom, all air-conditioned, with warm wooden ceilings and designer ceiling fans. The master suite has a large built-in wardrobe and its own bathroom with a freestanding bathtub 🛁, double vanity and an elegant walk-in rain shower. Three bathrooms in total.

📍 Choeng Mon Beach is about 5 minutes away, the airport 10, Fisherman's Village 12 and Chaweng's beach and centre 12–15, with restaurants, cafés and plenty to do in between. Back at the villa, the quiet residential setting lets you truly unwind.

🛜 Fast fibre internet throughout the villa.

🧹🧺 Housekeeping is included twice a week on stays of 7 nights or more. Extra cleaning is available on request.

## Guest access
The whole villa is yours: bedrooms, living areas, kitchen, pool, pool lounge and garden, with private parking behind the gate. Nothing is shared. Our team welcomes you in person, shows you around and hands over everything you need. The pool and garden team visits twice a week, always announced in advance and kept brief.

## Interaction with guests
We are a small hospitality team based on Samui, and we look after this villa and its guests ourselves. Before you arrive, we're happy to help plan your stay and arrange your airport pick-up. During your stay, we're a quick message away, with someone nearby if anything needs attention. Ask us anything: our favourite restaurants and beaches, a car or scooter delivered to the door, island and boat tours, or a surprise for a special occasion. Otherwise, we leave you to enjoy your privacy.
